# Orders & Customer Support

## Changing or cancelling an order

- An order can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of being placed, if it has not been dispatched.
- After dispatch, a cancellation is handled as a return, as described in Returns & Refunds. The delivery fee is not refunded.
- The delivery address can be changed before dispatch only. After dispatch the carrier may redirect the parcel for AED 20, if the new address is in a served area.
- To change an item or a quantity, cancel the order and place a new one.

## Order status

- A confirmation email is sent when the order is placed, and a dispatch email with the tracking number when the parcel leaves the warehouse.
- Order numbers look like #1042. Tracking numbers use the format TRK followed by four digits.
- Support can confirm the status from the order number.

## Promo codes and gift cards

- One promo code per order. Codes cannot be combined.
- Codes have no cash value and cannot be applied after the order is placed. The order must be cancelled and placed again within 1 hour.
- Gift cards are valid for 12 months from purchase and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Contacting support

- WhatsApp: +971 4 000 0000 (text messages only).
- Email: support@darnour.example.
- Hours: Monday to Friday 09:00 to 18:00, Saturday 10:00 to 14:00, Gulf Standard Time. Closed on Sunday and public holidays.
- The average first reply arrives within 4 business hours.
- Have the order number ready. Never share full card numbers.

## Escalation

- If an issue is unresolved after 5 business days, ask for a supervisor.
- Complaints are acknowledged within 1 business day and resolved within 10 business days.
- Complaints can also be sent to complaints@darnour.example.

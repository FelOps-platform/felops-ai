# Payments & Invoices

## Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express.
- Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- Cash on delivery: AED 15 fee, available for orders up to AED 1,500.
- Bank transfer: available for orders above AED 5,000. The order is dispatched after the funds clear, usually within 1 to 2 business days.

## Currency and taxes

- All prices are in United Arab Emirates dirhams (AED) and include 5% VAT.
- Orders in GCC countries are charged in AED, and the customer's bank may add a currency conversion fee.

## When an invoice is issued

- A tax invoice in PDF format is issued automatically when the order is dispatched. It is not issued when the order is placed.
- Invoice numbers use the format INV followed by four digits, for example INV-2210.
- The invoice is emailed to the address on the order and can also be requested from support.
- If an invoice is needed before dispatch, support can issue a proforma invoice. The tax invoice is still issued only after dispatch.

## Requesting a copy or correcting an invoice

- A copy or a correction (company name, tax registration number or address) can be requested within 30 days.
- Corrections take up to 2 business days. When an amount changes, a credit note and a new invoice are issued.
- The invoice date cannot be changed.

## Payment security and failed payments

- Card details are processed by our payment provider. Dar Nour does not store full card numbers.
- If a payment fails, the order stays unpaid for 24 hours and is then cancelled automatically.
- We never ask for card details over WhatsApp or email.

## Refund method

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method only, as described in Returns & Refunds.

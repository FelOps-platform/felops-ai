# Warranty & Repairs

## What is covered

- 12 months from delivery against manufacturing defects on furniture, lighting and decor.
- 6 months on electrical components, such as built-in LED lamps and USB ports.
- Structural faults, faulty hinges and electrical failures under normal use are covered.

## What is not covered

- Normal wear, scratches, fading and stains.
- Damage caused by misuse, incorrect assembly, water or heat.
- Items bought as clearance or marked "as is" on the product page.
- Commercial use.

## How to claim

- Contact support with the order number, photos and a short description. For electrical faults, include a short video.
- The assessment takes 3 to 5 business days. We may ask for the item to be returned, or send a technician in Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman.
- The remedy is repair first. If repair is not possible within 15 business days we replace the item. If no replacement is available we refund it.
- A warranty claim does not extend the original warranty period.
- Dar Nour does not sell an extended warranty.

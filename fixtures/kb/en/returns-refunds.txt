# Returns & Refunds Policy

Most products can be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. This policy explains the conditions, who pays for return shipping and how refunds are issued.

## Return window

- A return must be requested within 14 calendar days of delivery.
- The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with tags and all accessories.
- Proof of purchase is the order number or the invoice number, which uses the format INV followed by four digits.

## Non-returnable items

- Personalised or monogrammed products.
- Clearance items marked as final sale.
- Furniture that has been assembled or installed.
- Gift cards.
- Hygiene-sensitive items, such as opened pillows and mattress protectors.

## How to start a return

- Contact support with the order number, the item and the reason. We issue a return authorisation number within 1 business day.
- Pack the item securely and write the return authorisation number on the box.
- Inside the UAE we arrange collection with our courier. Outside the UAE the customer arranges and pays for return shipping, unless the item is faulty.
- A return fee of AED 20 is deducted inside the UAE. The fee is waived when the item is damaged, defective or incorrect.

## Refunds

- We inspect the item within 2 business days of its arrival at the warehouse.
- Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. The bank may take a further 3 to 5 days to show the amount.
- Original delivery fees are refunded only when the whole order is returned because it was damaged, defective or incorrect.
- Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer, and the customer provides an IBAN.
- Store credit is available instead of a refund. It is issued within 1 business day and includes a 5% bonus.

## Exchanges

- Exchanges for another colour or size are subject to stock availability.
- We hold the replacement for 3 business days after the return arrives.
- Any price difference is charged or refunded.

## Damaged, defective or incorrect items

- Report the problem within 48 hours of delivery and include photos.
- We arrange free collection and send a replacement or refund the full amount, including delivery fees.
- Do not assemble damaged furniture before contacting us.
